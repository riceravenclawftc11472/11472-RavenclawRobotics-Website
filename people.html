RAVENCLAW ROBOTICS

Our PEOPLE

Ravenclaw Robotics is an FTC (FIRST Tech Challenge) robotics team based in Plano, Texas. Every year, we create a competitive robot to compete in games against other robots from other teams. Every one of our team members plays a role in the team, whether it's assembling the robot, creating 3D models, programming the robot to do certain tasks, planning events, and getting sponsorships. ​The team was created in 2016, making this our eleventh year in competition. Our team currently consists of 15 members.
